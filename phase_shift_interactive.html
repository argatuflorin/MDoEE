Student tasks — include screenshots in your report

  1. Choose R. Pause at 90°. Record the phase difference and take a screenshot.
  2. Choose R–L. Increase inductance and identify which waveform leads. Capture both plots.
  3. Choose R–C. Explain why the current reaches its peak before the voltage.
  4. Choose R–L–C. Change frequency until the circuit is close to resonance. Capture the phase angle near 0° and record the displayed resonance frequency.
  5. On either side of resonance, determine whether the circuit is inductive or capacitive. Supply two screenshots and a short explanation.

Questions: What physical quantities control φ? Why does phase shift change sign at resonance? What does “current leads voltage” mean in the waveform and phasor diagrams?
